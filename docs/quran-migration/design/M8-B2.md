- No SwiftUI `.inspector` anywhere. The ayah panel is a trailing column **inside the reader** (HStack: reader | divider | `AyahPanel`) on the Mac and iPad regular-width landscape; a sheet at compact width / portrait; on a Duo vertical fold exactly the trailing half (M7.3 §3, now buildable). Fixes B2 and gives iPad its side panel back.
- **Out:** tabletop bottom panel (unchanged), note editor / "Read more" (stay sheets), drag-to-resize (Decision 1), renaming coordinator `…Inspector…` members (Decision 2), any route/link change.

## 8. Risks
- **The pop (B2, M7.1):** no `.inspector` left; a plain HStack child cannot pop a navigation stack.
- **Nested `NavigationStack`:** `AyahPanel` wraps a `NavigationStack`; inside the reader it is nested in the Quran stack. Precedent: `TabletopBottomView` already embeds it inline. If the Mac merges its title into the window toolbar or the iPad shows a stray bar: fallback, `AyahPanel` skips its `NavigationStack` and `.navigationTitle` when `isInspector` and shows the reference in the stepper row (A owns `AyahPanel.swift`, so it is in-batch).
- **T11:** sizes from the reader's own area (below the nav bar, beside the sidebar), never the window; measured outside the column so opening cannot flip it (no flip-flop).
- **T12:** `FoldGeometry` stays the only fold code; second caller is the one M7.3 planned; adhkar untouched.
- **Hot path (M5.1):** opening re-runs the modifier, not `QuranReaderView.body`; the page re-lays out narrower once (expected; Mushaf `MushafPageUnit` uses its own measured size, so a spread may become one page beside the column).
- **Safe area:** the GeometryReader respects the safe area; if the Mushaf page ignores it, `content` still can; the column stays under the nav bar.
- **Quran text:** untouched; panel copy/share keep `ayah.text`.
