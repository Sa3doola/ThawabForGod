Cause: `QuranCommandTarget` was published with `focusedSceneValue` from inside the split view and never reached `QuranCommands`' `@FocusedValue`. [fixed: the target is handed to `QuranCommands` directly from `AppContainer`; with the Mac menu enabling per section, ⌘F opens Search, ⌘G the go-to field, ⌘D bookmarks; checked with real keystrokes on the Mac and by `testShortcuts*`, `testMacQuranMenuEnablement` on Mac and iPad]
