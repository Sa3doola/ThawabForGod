Tanzil Quran text: copyright notice and terms of use

Noor displays the Quran in the Uthmani text of the Tanzil Project (https://tanzil.net) and searches it
through the Tanzil Simple Clean text, both version 1.1. The text is used exactly as published: it is
copied into the app's database verbatim and never changed, except that the basmala Tanzil prefixes to
verse 1 of 112 surahs is moved to the surah's heading, which moves its words without altering them (a
SHA-256 over all the verses is checked when
the database is built). The source is named and linked in Settings > Sources. The notices below are
reproduced unchanged from the copies of the text the database was built from, and are also stored
inside the database itself (table "meta", keys "source:tanzil-uthmani:notice" and
"source:tanzil-simple-clean:notice").
