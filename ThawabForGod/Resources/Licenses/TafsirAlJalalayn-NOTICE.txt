Tafsir al-Jalalayn: status of the text

Tafsir al-Jalalayn (the Arabic commentary shown in the ayah panel) was begun by Jalal al-Din
al-Mahalli (d. 864 AH / 1459 CE) and completed by Jalal al-Din al-Suyuti (d. 911 AH / 1505 CE). The
work is in the public domain by age, and Noor claims no rights in it.

The Arabic text in the app was taken from the mirror https://github.com/spa5k/tafsir_api
(tafsir/ar-tafsir-al-jalalayn). The mirror is a transcription, not a licence: it is the age of the work,
not the host, that makes the text free to redistribute. The text is stored without editing (each note is
only escaped and wrapped in a paragraph for display; a SHA-256 of the notes is checked when the
database is built). 226 of the 6,236 verses have no note in this commentary, and the app says so
rather than showing another verse's note.

Nobody on this project has read the transcription line by line against a printed edition.

English translations of this commentary belong to their modern translators and are not included.
